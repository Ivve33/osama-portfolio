AirQuest

Turning airport wait time into a mission.

A prototype that pairs airport navigation with challenges and loyalty rewards for Riyadh Air passengers.

My role

Behind the project

The idea

Passengers have stretches of waiting time between airport tasks. We explored how short activities could help them discover the terminal and make that time more engaging.

What I contributed

I worked with a multidisciplinary team to develop and present the prototype. The pitch brought navigation, location-based missions, and loyalty rewards into one passenger journey.

How we designed it

The concept uses location to suggest activities near the passenger and explores how AI personalization and AR could support that experience.
