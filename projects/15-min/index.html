Behind the project

The idea

Build a small room where clues in the environment invite the player to look more closely and try things out.

What I built

I created a playable 3D prototype in Unity, including the room, interactive puzzles, and the countdown logic in C#.

How I designed it

The countdown gives the player an immediate goal. The room and puzzles carry the rest of the experience through exploration and interaction.
